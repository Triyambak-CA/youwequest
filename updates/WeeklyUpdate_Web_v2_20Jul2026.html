Direct Tax · Notification 85/2026, 15 July 2026 · Material
Cost Inflation Index for FY 2026-27 set at 384
The CBDT notified the Cost Inflation Index (CII) at 384 for FY / Tax Year 2026-27 (AY 2027-28), up from 376 for FY 2025-26 - a 2.13% rise - under s.72(8)(a) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Use CII = 384 for the indexed cost of acquisition / improvement when computing long-term capital gains on transfers made on or after 1 April 2026, for the assets on which indexation is still available (it was withdrawn for most assets from 23 July 2024).

02
RBI / FEMA · A.P. (DIR Series) Circular 19, 17 July 2026 · Material
Special Rupee Vostro Account framework consolidated into one circular
The RBI (RBI/2026-27/203) consolidated and rationalised all prior instructions on Special Rupee Vostro Accounts (SRVAs), permitting AD banks to open SRVAs for cross-border trade settlement in Indian Rupees and permissible current- and capital-account transactions. It supersedes five earlier circulars (2022-2025). For clients using INR trade settlement, re-map FEMA documentation and compliance checklists to this single reference - the earlier five circulars are withdrawn.
Source ↗
03
ICAI · Announcement, 15 July 2026 · Material
ICAI (Global Networking) Guidelines, 2025 kept in abeyance
The ICAI has kept further implementation of the ICAI (Global Networking) Guidelines, 2025 in abeyance until further orders (Aggregation of CA Firms Committee announcement, 15 July 2026). The Guidelines - notified vide Notification No. 3-CACAF/GN-F/2026 dated 11 February 2026 (Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part III, Section 4, dated 17 February 2026) - were to govern tie-ups between Indian CA firms and global networks. Firms with, or planning, global-network affiliations should hold off on Guideline-driven restructuring and await the revised framework.

04
Direct Tax · Notification 88/2026, 16 July 2026
Protocol amending the India-Sri Lanka DTAA given effect in India
Under s.159(1) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, the CBDT gave effect in India to the Protocol amending the India-Sri Lanka DTAA (signed 16 December 2024), which entered into force on 19 June 2026. The amended provisions apply in India to income of fiscal years beginning on or after 1 April 2027 (AY 2028-29). International-tax practices should update treaty positions, TRC / Form 10F and withholding analysis for India-Sri Lanka flows.

Direct Tax · Notification 84/2026, 15 July 2026
Oman's Social Protection Fund notified under s.10(23FE)
The CBDT notified Oman's Social Protection Fund (SPF) (PAN ABOCS3010J) as a specified pension fund eligible for the s.10(23FE) exemption on eligible Indian investments made up to 31 March 2030 - dividend, interest and long-term capital gains from eligible infrastructure investments are exempt, subject to the prescribed reporting and continued Oman regulation. Relevant to inbound structuring and the withholding position on payments to this fund.

Direct Tax · TRACES 2.0 portal update
OLTAS challan correction for TY 2025 & 2026 coming to TRACES 2.0
TRACES (CPC-TDS) has notified that OLTAS challan correction for FY / TY 2025-26 and TY 2026-27 will be enabled shortly on TRACES 2.0. Deductors will then be able to submit online OLTAS correction requests (challan-detail corrections - assessment year, major / minor head, amount) for these tax years through the portal's correction wizard. Await the enablement before initiating challan corrections for the current cycle rather than routing them offline.

Reference

Scope

Item 05 · Oman Social Protection Fund · Direct Tax · new law
05.1
Reference
Notification No. 84/2026 (S.O. 3890(E); F.No. 500/PF-15/S10(23FE)/FT&TR-II) dated 15 July 2026, under s.10(23FE) of the Income-tax Act, 1961.
05.2
Scope
Notifies Oman's Social Protection Fund (SPF) (PAN ABOCS3010J) as a specified pension fund whose eligible Indian investments made up to 31 March 2030 qualify for the s.10(23FE) exemption on dividend, interest and long-term capital gains from eligible infrastructure investments.
05.3
Action
Inbound-structuring and withholding relevance - payments to the SPF on eligible investments carry the exemption, subject to the fund satisfying the prescribed conditions (continued Oman regulation, prescribed reporting, no loan / benefit to participants beyond the pension mandate). Verify eligibility of each investment before applying nil / reduced withholding.
Item 06 · TRACES 2.0 OLTAS correction · Direct Tax · portal note
06.1
What changed
A TRACES (CPC-TDS) portal notice states that OLTAS challan correction for FY / TY 2025-26 and TY 2026-27 will be enabled shortly on TRACES 2.0. The online OLTAS correction request flow (enter challan details for which the correction is sought) is being extended to these tax years.
06.2
Context
Follows the migration to TRACES 2.0. OLTAS correction covers changes to challan attributes - assessment year, major / minor head, and amount - previously routed through the assessing officer or the older utility.
06.3
Action
Where a deductor needs to correct a challan (wrong AY, head or amount) for FY / TY 2025-26 or TY 2026-27, await the TRACES 2.0 enablement and use the online facility rather than an offline route; monitor the portal for the go-live.
